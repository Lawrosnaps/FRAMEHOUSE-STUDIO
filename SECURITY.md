# Security policy

This repository holds planning documents for SHFTRS, an app run by
SHFTRS Ltd that holds shift, leave and wellbeing records for police
officers and staff. If you think you have found a security problem in
the app, its service or this repository, we want to hear from you, and
we will treat your report seriously and in confidence.

## How to report

Either:

- use the **Report a vulnerability** button on this repository's
  **Security** tab, or
- email **response@shftrs.app**.

Please tell us what you found and where, how to reproduce it, and what
you think someone could do with it.

Please do not open a public issue, pull request or discussion about a
security problem.

## What happens next

- We will acknowledge your report within 3 working days.
- We will tell you whether we can reproduce it, and what we plan to do,
  within 10 working days.
- We will keep you updated until it is fixed.
- If personal data was affected, we will handle it under our
  data-protection obligations, which may include telling the Information
  Commissioner's Office and the people affected.

## Testing rules

- Use only accounts you created yourself. Do not access, change or
  delete anyone else's data. If you reach someone else's data by
  accident, stop, do not keep a copy, and tell us.
- No denial-of-service, spam or social engineering of our users or team.
- Problems in services we use but do not run (such as Supabase, Expo,
  Apple, Google or GitHub) should go to that provider.
- Give us a reasonable time to fix the problem before you tell anyone
  else.

If you act in good faith and follow these rules, we will not take legal
action against you over your research.

There is no bug bounty. With your permission, we are glad to thank you
by name once the problem is fixed.
